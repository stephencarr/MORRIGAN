MORRIGAN Mobile 0.2.0 prototype

Original MORRIGAN implementation: GPL-3.0-only; full licence in MORRIGAN-COPYING.txt.
Corresponding source, native build scripts and pinned dependency fetch instructions:
https://github.com/stephencarr/MORRIGAN/tree/morrigan-mobile/pixel9

The application incorporates the original LIRA-8 Pure Data patch by Miguel Moreno,
libpd, and Pure Data by Miller Puckette and contributors. Their BSD-style licences
and copyright notices are included beside this notice. Exact source revisions are
recorded in the packaged sources.json and the source preparation script.

The LIRA synthesis graph is unmodified. MORRIGAN supplies native control/audio
integration, output safety limiting and an original Android user interface.
Upstream LIRA outputs mono duplicated to two channels.

The interface is original and uses Android's system monospace font. No Macintosh
ROM, operating-system software, font, icon or other extracted Apple asset is used.

Erica Synths, or the upstream software authors.
